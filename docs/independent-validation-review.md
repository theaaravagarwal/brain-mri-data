## Required before the next evaluation

- A new source or cohort with permission for the intended research use.
- T1, T1ce, T2, and FLAIR files for each case, plus expert masks for scoring.
- Provenance and overlap checks against development data and the previous
  60-case cohort. Different filenames alone do not establish independence.
- A frozen manifest with file hashes, inclusion/exclusion rules, and expected
  case count recorded before inference.
- The unchanged serving checkpoint, preprocessing, and threshold. Record their
  exact identifiers alongside the evaluation; do not select cases by results.
- Aggregate metrics including empty outputs, failure counts, and uncertainty.

Use the existing app for individual cases and preserve its receipt. Keep any
case-level review material in private storage, not in this repository. The
existing 60-case PDF remains the released evaluation report until a separately
identified evaluation is completed.

- Extra regions or boundary disagreement:

Human inspection requires a local image viewer capable of displaying the scan,
model mask, and reference together. The metadata explanation does not perform
that inspection. Leave unknown fields unassessed rather than inferring them
from Dice or the language output.

## Separate operational item

Service startup without login is configured on `.1` and `.7`, but an actual
reboot recovery test remains unperformed. Such a test temporarily interrupts
the prototype and should be scheduled explicitly. It is separate from assessing
segmentation quality and cannot substitute for independent data or expert review.
